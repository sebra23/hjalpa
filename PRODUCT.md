# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Swedish small service-business owners and the people presenting the Svarar voice-agent demo to them. Visitors need to understand the offer quickly, enter company details safely, and test the assistant without learning a new tool.

## Product Purpose

Svarar turns a company's own details into a personalized Millis voice-agent demo. Success is a visitor completing the company form, saving a demo profile, and starting the voice test with clear expectations about metadata and microphone access.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and editorial. The interface should feel like a product magazine spread rather than a conventional software dashboard. Regular serif typography carries the identity, supported by warm-gray, white, and black surfaces, a mint voice panel, sparse yellow micro-accents, and carefully composed product artifacts.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, heavy dashboard shells, lifestyle stock photography, decorative performance metrics, repeated feature-card grids, bold display typography, and colorful illustrations that distract from the voice test.

## Design Principles

- Make the company-specific voice demo the central action.
- Use dramatic type scale without delaying access to the form.
- Keep the visual system quiet, spacious, and editorial, with elevation reserved for product artifacts.
- Treat privacy, validation, loading, and failure states as part of the design.
- Preserve the existing Swedish copy voice and predictable workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic form labels, reduced-motion support, readable mobile type, and touch-friendly controls. Do not rely on color alone for validation or status.
